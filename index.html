Why brands source from us

Mill capability, without the trading layer

01

Mill-direct sourcing

Our partner mills in Guangdong and Fujian knit, dye and finish the fabric. We are the English-speaking front end and the quality control — not a reseller buying from a reseller.

02

Specs you can hold us to

Composition, weight, width, elongation and recovery are stated per quality and tested against the approved sample. Vague answers are how supply problems start.

03

Colour matched, not guessed

Send a Pantone, a swatch or an existing garment. We run lab dips for approval before bulk, and keep the approved standard on file for repeat orders.

04

Certification with paperwork

OEKO-TEX and GRS are available through the partner mills. A recycled claim comes with a transaction certificate — without one, the claim is worthless to your buyer.
