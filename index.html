What we do for you

Four things that decide whether the fabric works

Anyone can quote a price per kilo. What decides whether a fabric works in production is specification, colour control, testing before dyeing, and documentation your buyer accepts.

01

Specification

Composition, weight, width, elongation and recovery written per quality and tested against the approved sample — not summarised as "high stretch".

02

Lab dip & colour matching

Pantone TCX, a swatch or an existing garment. Two rounds of correction included, and the approved standard is kept on file for repeats.

03

Testing before bulk

Opacity, pilling, colourfastness and shrinkage run on the sample yardage, because the cheapest correction happens before the dye bath.

04

Certification & documents

OEKO-TEX and GRS where the partner mill holds the certificate, with export documents that match what the certificate actually covers.

Why brands source from us

Mill capability, without the trading layer

01

Mill-direct sourcing

Our partner mills in Guangdong and Fujian knit, dye and finish the fabric. We are the English-speaking front end and the quality control — not a reseller buying from a reseller.

02

Specs you can hold us to

Composition, weight, width, elongation and recovery are stated per quality and tested against the approved sample. Vague answers are how supply problems start.

03

Colour matched, not guessed

Send a Pantone, a swatch or an existing garment. We run lab dips for approval before bulk and keep the approved standard on file for repeat orders.

04

Certification with paperwork

OEKO-TEX and GRS are available through the partner mills. A recycled claim comes with a transaction certificate — without one, the claim is worthless to your buyer.
