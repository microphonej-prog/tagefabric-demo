Buyer questions

Brushed Fleece & French Terry — what buyers ask

Cotton or polyester fleece?

Cotton gives the soft, heavy hand most premium lifestyle sets want. Poly-cotton is cheaper, dries faster and holds colour well. Recycled polyester is the option brands with ESG targets ask for.

What GSM for a hoodie?

280–320 gsm is the commercial range for a substantial hoodie. Below 240 gsm reads as a light sweatshirt, which is a different product and a different price point.

Does it pill?

Brushed surfaces are more prone to pilling than flat knits. We specify the pilling test on the approved sample and adjust the brushing pass rather than promising it cannot happen.

What is the minimum order?

300 kg per colour dyed-to-order; stock shades in smaller lengths for sampling.
