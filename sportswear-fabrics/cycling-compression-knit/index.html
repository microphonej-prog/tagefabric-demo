Buyer questions

High-Density Cycling Compression Knit — what buyers ask

What GSM should bib shorts be?

230–260 gsm is the working range for the short panel. Below that you lose opacity under stretch; above it the fabric becomes stiff and the panel seam starts to irritate.

How is abrasion resistance tested?

Martindale abrasion to the cycle count your customer specifies. Ask for the number, not the word 'durable' — that is the difference between a spec and an adjective.

Can it take silicone gripper tape?

Yes. Gripper application is a finishing step; tell us the tape type and width and we will confirm compatibility with the mill before bulk.

What is the minimum order?

500 kg per colour dyed-to-order; compression qualities are dyed in dedicated runs.
