ODM activewear development — you bring the brief

Start from a shape that already works, then change the fabric, the length, the waistband, the colour and the details to make it yours. Lower development cost, faster to market, and no pattern library needed on day one.

Is ODM the right route?

Choose ODM when you want to move faster

You are ready for ODM if

You are launching a label, adding a capsule range, or testing whether a category is worth a full development budget. You know the customer, the price point and the look — but not yet the pattern, the fabric code or the construction.

How development works

We start from a block: leggings, a sports bra, a training tee, a hoodie, a swimsuit. You pick the fabric from our knit range (or we develop one), we adjust the length, rise, support level and trims, then sample. Two fit rounds are included; further rounds are quoted.

What stays yours

The developed style is exclusive to you for the season. We do not sell the same combination of block, fabric and trim detail to another buyer.

What you can change

Enough variables to make it a different garment

Fabric

Nude-feel brushed, 15D/20D ultrafine, high-compression 85/15, GRS recycled, ribbed, Ponte Roma, moisture-wicking jersey, chlorine-resistant swim, fleece.

Fit & rise

High, mid or low rise; 7/8, full or bike length; gusset construction; waistband depth and bonding style.

Support level

Light, medium or high support bras — elastic band, bonded edge, removable cups, power-mesh lining.

Details

Pockets (side, hidden waistband, phone), flatlock vs bonded seams, contouring panels, reflective hits.

Colour & print

Pantone TCX lab dips, stock shades for speed, sublimation prints, tonal jacquard and yarn-dye stripes.

Branding

Woven labels, care labels, size tabs, hang tags, poly bags and paper bags — produced in-house and packed with the garment.

Cost reality

What ODM does and does not save

Saves

Pattern development, grading and the first two fit rounds are far cheaper than starting from zero. You skip the six-week block-development phase.

Does not save

Fabric development, if you want a knit that does not exist yet, is charged the same as any custom development — dyed-to-order from 300 kg per colour.

Exclusivity

Style exclusivity applies to the exact combination you develop. The underlying block remains in our range, which is what keeps ODM affordable.
