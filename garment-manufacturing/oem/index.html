OEM apparel production — you own the design

Send a tech pack that is ready to cut and we produce it to your pattern, in your fabric direction, under your label. You keep the fit and the intellectual property; we own the schedule and the quality gate.

Is OEM the right route?

Choose OEM when the design is already yours

You are ready for OEM if you have

A graded pattern or an existing production file, a measurement sheet, construction details (seam types, stitch density, bonding where used), and a clear fabric direction. Brands that have already sold the style somewhere make the smoothest OEM customers, because the fit is settled and the arguments are about production, not design.

What we need from you

Tech pack with flat sketches and points of measure, a reference sample in your target fit, artwork for labels and hang tags, and the invoice/packing requirements for your market. If any of that is missing we will tell you which gap will delay the line.

What we handle

Fabric sourcing and inspection, cutting, sewing, trims attachment, pressing, folding, bagging, carton marks and the AQL inspection report. If the knit comes from our range, colour and hand-feel are settled before cutting instead of after.

Production detail

What the line controls

Pattern & grading

We cut from your pattern file. If grading is missing we can have it done, but it is quoted separately and approved by you before cutting.

Fabric inspection

Four-point inspection on incoming rolls, with shade banding checked against the approved lab dip. Customer-supplied fabric is inspected before cutting, not after.

Sewing & bonding

Overlock, flatlock, coverstitch, twin-needle, bartack and hot-melt bonding for seamless-look hems and waistbands.

Trims & branding

Woven labels, care labels, size tabs, hang tags and poly bags — produced in-house by our accessories division and attached to your spec, not a stock approximation.

Quality gate

AQL 2.5 inspection on measurements, symmetry, seam strength, colour and trims. Report emailed with photographs before the cartons are sealed.

Shipping

FOB Shenzhen/Yantian or EXW, with carton marks and packing lists prepared for your forwarder. Air freight for sample-level volumes.

Common questions

Before you send the pack

Can you work from a reference garment only?

For simple shapes, yes — we can build a pattern from a sample. For constructed activewear (bonded waistbands, moulded cups, gussets) a proper pattern saves a fit round and is usually cheaper than reverse-engineering.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes, before a tech pack is shared. Your pattern and artwork stay yours, and we do not produce the same style for another buyer.

Can I use my own fabric supplier?

Yes. We inspect it on arrival and will flag shrinkage or shade risk before cutting. If the roll count is short, we say so before the line starts.
