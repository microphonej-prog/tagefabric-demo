Cut-and-sew garment manufacturing for activewear brands

Yoga leggings, sports bras, training tees, hoodies and swim — produced in Dongguan with the fabric supplied by the same team. One supplier for knit and garment means fewer handoffs, faster sampling, and a cost line you can actually calculate.

Why one supply chain matters

Fabric in-house, sewing next door

The problem with buying fabric and garments separately

A brand normally buys the knit from one supplier and gives it to a sewing factory. When the fabric arrives late, the sewing slot is lost. When the hand-feel is wrong, nobody owns it. When the shade does not match the lab dip, both sides point at each other.

We sit across both steps. The knit is specified, dyed and finished in the same partner-mill network that feeds our sewing lines in Guangdong — and the labels, hang tags and packaging bags come from the same group, so a finished garment can ship with its trims already attached and bagged.

What that changes in practice

Sampling is faster because fabric and garment are scheduled together. Colour and hand-feel problems get solved once, before cutting. And because there is no trading layer between the mill and the sewing floor, the price you see has fewer margins stacked inside it.

Two ways to work with us

OEM or ODM — pick the one that matches what you already have

OEM — you bring the design

You own the pattern, the fit and the tech pack. We source the fabric, cut, sew, finish and pack to your specification. Best for brands with a developed range that needs a reliable production base.

OEM production details →

ODM — you bring the brief

You pick from our activewear blocks and we develop the fabric, fit and details with you. Faster to market and lower development cost. Best for new labels, capsule ranges and test runs.

ODM development details →

Mixed — the usual case

Most brands arrive with one developed style and a rough idea for the rest. We keep the developed style as OEM and develop the new ones as ODM, all produced on the same line with the same fabric.

How a program runs

Six steps from tech pack to delivered cartons

Tech pack review

Pattern, measurements, construction details and any reference garment. We reply with what is buildable, what is not, and where cost will land.

Fabric confirmation

If the knit is ours, we lock composition, weight and hand-feel first. If you supply fabric, we inspect it before cutting.

Fit sample

7 to 10 working days. One style, one colour, photographed on a mannequin with measurement points marked.

Fit corrections

Round two and three as needed. We keep the pattern file versioned so the approved fit is what the line actually sews.

Bulk production

25 to 35 days after approval. Cutting, sewing, trims attachment, pressing, folding, bagging, carton labelling.

Inspection & shipping

AQL inspection against your measurement sheet and the approved sample, with the report sent before the cartons leave.

The numbers

Stated plainly, before you ask

MOQ

300 pcs per style per colour. Below that, sampling is possible but bulk production is not economic — we will say so rather than take the order and disappoint you.

Sampling cost

Charged per style and credited against bulk orders above 500 pcs. Fabric development is charged separately if a new knit is required.

Lead time

7–10 working days for first fit, 25–35 days for bulk after approval. Peak season (Aug–Nov) adds a week — book early.

Order size we suit best

500–10,000 pcs per style per run. Smaller runs are possible on stock fabrics; above 10,000 pcs we plan capacity in advance.

Certification

OEKO-TEX and GRS available where the partner factory holds the certificate and can issue a TC. We name the specific certificate on the quote.

Factory contact

Video walkthrough or an in-person visit to the sewing floor and the mill. We would rather you saw it than took our word for it.
