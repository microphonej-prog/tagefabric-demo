About TAGE FABRIC

Yoga and activewear knits, sourced mill-direct from Guangdong and Fujian for brands in the United States and Europe.

Where this came from

TAGE started in garment trims — hang tags, woven labels, care labels and packaging for apparel brands. Working with those brands, the same question kept coming back: can you also help us source the fabric?

So we built the fabric side around the mills our own network already trusts — knitwear mills in Guangdong and Fujian, several of them run by people we grew up with. That is not a marketing story, it is how the quality control actually works: we can call the dye house directly and get an answer.

How we work

We are the front end, not the factory. The mills knit, dye and finish; we specify, translate, inspect and chase. You get one English-speaking contact, one approved standard, and one place to send a complaint.

We would rather lose an order than ship a fabric that will fail your opacity test in week three. Repeat business from six brands is worth more than one urgent container.

Where we ship

United States and Canada, the United Kingdom and the EU, plus Japan. Air freight for sample and trial quantities, sea freight for bulk. Export documents and test reports travel with the shipment.
