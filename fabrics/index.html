Fabric range — yoga and sportswear knits

Thirteen qualities in two lines. Yoga fabrics for studio and training leggings; sportswear fabrics for running, swim, cycling, training and warm-up. Composition and weight stated on every page.

Two lines

Choose the line, then the quality

Yoga fabrics are about hand-feel and opacity at the legging. Sportswear fabrics are about the performance claim the garment makes — wicking, chlorine resistance, abrasion, warmth.

Yoga fabrics

Leggings are not one fabric problem. A buttery studio legging, a squat-proof training tight and a flared pant need different structures — pairing the wrong one is the most common sourcing mistake we see.

Sportswear fabrics

Sportswear is a wear test. A jersey that stops wicking, a swim knit that goes slack, a bib short that abrades at the inner thigh — the buyer comes back, and the fabric is why.
