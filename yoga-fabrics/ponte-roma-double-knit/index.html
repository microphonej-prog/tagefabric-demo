Buyer questions

Ponte Roma Double Knit — what buyers ask

Is Ponte Roma suitable for yoga practice?

For low-impact practice, walking and studio-to-street wear, yes. It has cross-grain stretch but limited lengthwise stretch, so it is not the right choice for deep sequencing or high-compression training — that is why ranges pair ponte bottoms with a compression or nude-feel legging.

How does Ponte Roma differ from jersey?

Jersey is a single knit and drapes softly with high stretch. Ponte is a double knit: two sets of needles, heavier, more stable, and much less prone to clinging. It behaves closer to a woven in construction while staying comfortable.

Will it pill?

Double knits are more pill-resistant than brushed single knits, but friction points still matter. We specify the pilling test on the approved sample rather than assuming.
