Buyer questions

GRS Recycled Nylon Spandex — what buyers ask

Do you actually hold GRS certification?

Our partner mills hold GRS certification, and we supply the certificate scope plus a transaction certificate (TC) for each shipment. If a specific mill's certificate has lapsed, we say so and offer the virgin equivalent instead — we will not sell an unverified recycled claim.

What is a transaction certificate and why does it matter?

A TC is the document that links a specific shipment to certified recycled content. Buyers and certifiers audit it. A mill that cannot issue a TC can sell you 'recycled' fibre, but you cannot legally claim GRS content on the finished product.

Does recycled nylon cost more?

Yes — typically a premium over virgin nylon. It is the single most common reason brands drop recycled programmes. Our job is to keep the premium predictable and to confirm the claim is documentable before you commit to a collection.

What is the minimum order?

300 kg per colour dyed-to-order. GRS-documented orders need the TC request raised at order confirmation, not after shipment.
