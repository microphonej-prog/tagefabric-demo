Apparel supply chain services — swatch to carton

Fabric, trims, garment production, inspection and export documents, run by one team in Dongguan. You keep the design and the customer; we take the coordination risk that normally lands on a brand when four suppliers point at each other.

The problem we remove

Four suppliers, four ways to lose a season

Fragmented sourcing is not inefficient, it is expensive in ways that never appear on a quote sheet.

The fabric arrives late

The sewing slot is gone and the factory has moved to another client. You pay for air freight to recover three weeks, or you slip the launch.

The shade does not match

The mill blames the dye lot, the sewing factory blames the mill, and nobody who could have fixed it is in the same conversation.

The hand-feel is wrong

Nobody notices until the samples are sewn. By then the fabric is cut and the money is spent.

The trims do not arrive together

Labels, care labels and bags land after the garments are finished, so packing is delayed by a component worth cents.

Scope

What we actually handle

1 · Material sourcing & testing

Thirteen knit qualities in production plus development to your spec. Composition, weight, width, elongation, recovery and colourfastness tested against the approved standard before cutting.

2 · Pattern & fit development

Pattern making, grading, block adaptation and fit engineering. Where you have a reference garment, we work from measurements and construction details rather than assumptions.

3 · Sampling

First fit in 7–10 working days, photographed on a mannequin with measurement points marked. Two to three rounds until signed off; the pattern is version-locked afterwards so the line sews what you approved.

4 · Production management

Cut, sew, bond, print, embroider, press, fold, bag, label the carton — scheduled as one run from 300 pcs per style per colour, with the capacity booked before your season starts.

5 · Quality control

AQL inspection against your measurement sheet and the approved sample. Reports are sent before cartons leave, and defects are reworked rather than shipped with an explanation.

6 · Trims, packing & documents

Woven labels, care labels, hang tags, poly and paper bags from our accessories division, attached and packed before shipping, with the commercial invoice, packing list and test reports your broker needs.

What stays with you

Design ownership and the pattern files. The customer relationship and the brand. The decision on price positioning. We do not sell under our own label into your market, and we do not contact your accounts.

What we take off you

Chasing the dye house. Re-checking the shade against the lab dip. Booking the sewing slot before the fabric lands. Photographing the fit sample from four angles. Writing the inspection report. Filing the export documents correctly the first time.

Why this structure works

Fabric, trims and sewing sit in one network with one schedule. When something slips, the person who has to fix it is the person who planned it — not a supplier who can plausibly deny responsibility.

Buyer questions

What brands ask first

Do you own the mills and the sewing lines?

The mills are long-standing partners in Guangdong and Fujian; the sewing is done by partner factories whose lines we schedule; the accessories division is ours. We say partner, not owned, because a buyer who visits should find exactly what we described.

Can I use my own fabric or my own pattern?

Yes to both. Incoming fabric is inspected against the approved standard before cutting, and we will tell you if it is likely to fail in sewing or after washing. Your pattern files stay yours.

What happens if the bulk fails inspection?

It is reworked or remade. The approved sample is the standard, and the inspection report exists so that the conversation is about facts rather than opinions. That is also why fabric is tested before cutting.

How do you price it?

Fabric per kg, garment per piece, development per style. Every quote states MOQ, lead time and which certification applies to that fabric and factory, so no extra line appears later.
